# Namaste-Chef

Welcome to **Namaste-Chef**, your go-to destination for authentic regional recipes from six different states of India! Embark on a culinary journey across the diverse food culture of India with recipes from Maharashtra, Gujarat, Karnataka, Punjab, Tamil Nadu, and Goa. Whether you're a food enthusiast or a home chef, our website offers a delightful range of traditional dishes that bring the true flavors of each state to your kitchen.

## Website Overview

### Landing Page

The landing page is the heart of Namaste-Chef, where we warmly welcome you with the vibrant energy of Indian cuisine. 

- **Website Name**: Centered in the middle of the page, the website name is prominently displayed, giving you a clear sense of our brand.
- **Background**: A visually appetizing background featuring delicious Indian dishes sets the tone, making you eager to dive into the culinary adventure.

![Landing Page](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/aff50191-a4e6-4086-89e1-7defc23f6b9e)

#### State Selection

Down the page, you will find clickable options of six Indian states, each representing its own rich and diverse food culture: 

- Maharashtra
- Punjab
- Goa
- Tamil Nadu
- Gujarat
- Karnataka

Each state card comes with a **“Let’s Cook”** button that directs you to authentic recipes from that particular state, helping you explore the local culinary heritage.

## How to Explore

1. **Select Your State**: Choose a state from the landing page to begin your culinary journey.
2. **Authentic Recipes**: Click on the "Let’s Cook" button to access a wide range of traditional recipes.
3. **Cook Like a Local**: Each recipe is a deep dive into the flavors of the selected state, complete with step-by-step instructions and traditional cooking tips.

## Why Namaste-Chef?

Namaste-Chef stands out for offering authentic, regional Indian recipes that are true to their origins. We aim to preserve the heritage of each state's cuisine while making it accessible to home cooks around the world. Every dish comes with a cultural essence, connecting you to India through the joy of cooking and tasting.
